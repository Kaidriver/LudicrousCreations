  • This watch stand is perfect for showcasing all of your exquisite watches! The simplistic and light design makes sure that your watch will stand out and shine. The loop of the stand measures at 2.25 inches in diameter, meaning most of your watches will be able to fit on this stand. The loop also has a hole that will perfectly fit an Apple Watch charger, allowing you to charge your watch while it’s on display! The whole stand measures at a height of 3 inches, with a width (measured from the base) of 2 inches and depth of 2.6 inches. The watch stand also comes in a variety of colors, with special requests also accepted (at an additional charge)!
